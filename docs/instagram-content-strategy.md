# Spinzo Instagram & Social Media Strategy
**Goal:** Build brand mentions → drive AI citations (3x stronger than backlinks)
**Current state:** @spinzolaundry — 3 followers, 7 posts, last post Feb 2026

## Why This Matters for SEO

Ahrefs' 2025 study of 75,000 brands found that **brand mentions on social platforms correlate ~3x more strongly with AI citations than backlinks do**. YouTube mentions had the strongest correlation (r=0.737), followed by Reddit. For Spinzo, this is the single biggest off-site gap.

## Growth Tactics

### 1. Bangalore Micro-Influencers (5 partnerships)
Find 5 Bangalore micro-influencers (5K-20K followers) in these niches:
- 2 Bangalore lifestyle/food bloggers (they have engaged local audiences)
- 2 Bangalore tech/startup accounts (target your core demographic)
- 1 Bangalore home/family account (families = high laundry volume)

**Offer:** 3 months free service (worth ~₹5,000) in exchange for 1 honest Instagram post + 1 story.

### 2. Hashtag Strategy
Primary (high volume): #bangalore #laundryservice #bangalorelaundry
Secondary (area-specific): #koramangala #btmlayout #hsrlayout #jayanagar
Niche (high intent): #washandfold #drycleaning #samedaylaundry #laundrypickup

### 3. Content Pillars (rotate these)
- **Speed:** 30-min pickup, 6-hr delivery
- **Price:** ₹85/kg transparency vs competitors
- **Quality:** Steam not coal iron, zero mixing guarantee
- **Coverage:** "We come to you" area by area
- **Before/After:** Stain removal transformations
